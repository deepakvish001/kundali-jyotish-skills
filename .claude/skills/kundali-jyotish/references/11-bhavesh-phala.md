# Bhavesh phala: lord of each house placed in each house (144 results)

Classical summaries in the spirit of BPHS (ch. 24), Phaladeepika and Saravali, rewritten in plain
language. Use them as the *base meaning*, then modify by: the lord's dignity and avastha, its
functional role for the Lagna, aspects/conjunctions, D9 strength, and the dasha that activates it.
A strong lord gives the good side; a weak or afflicted lord gives the difficult side.
Format: **Lh in Hn** - result.
