## 1. Choosing what to remedy

- **Strengthen** (gemstone, mantra, worship): functional benefics that are weak - Lagna lord,
  5th lord, 9th lord, yogakaraka, and the running MD/AD lord if it is a benefic for this Lagna.
  Weak = debilitated without full cancellation, combust, Sushupti/Mrita/Khala avastha, low
  Vimshopaka (<10) or low bala, in dusthana, heavily aspected by malefics.
- **Pacify** (mantra, daan, vrat - not gemstones): functional malefics and troublesome planets -
  lords of 6, 8, 12, the badhaka lord, Rahu/Ketu, Saturn during Sade Sati, any planet whose dasha
  or transit is currently hurting.
- Never prescribe the gemstone of a 6th/8th/12th lord or a functional malefic for the Lagna: it
  strengthens the troubles. Say so plainly if the report recommends one ("Report ne X ratna bataya
  hai, par aapke Lagna ke liye X ka swami 6th/8th ka hai, isliye ye ratna nahi").

Rules:
- Natural, untreated stone touching the skin. Energise before wearing: wash in raw milk and Ganga
  jal (or clean water), light a lamp, chant the planet's beej mantra 108 times in its hora on its
  day, preferably in Shukla paksha, then wear.
- Do not combine stones of natural enemies in one hand: Ruby with Blue sapphire/Diamond/Gomed;
  Pearl with Gomed/Cat's eye/Blue sapphire; Coral with Emerald/Gomed/Diamond; Yellow sapphire with
  Diamond/Emerald/Blue sapphire.
- **Blue sapphire, Gomed, Cat's eye** act fast and strongly: only when Saturn/Rahu/Ketu are
  functional benefics or placed in 3/6/11/kendra with good dignity, usually during their dasha.
  Traditionally test-wear for 3 days (keep under the pillow / tied to the arm) and stop if sleep,
  health or events turn bad.
- For a child or a budget constraint, prescribe the uparatna or the mantra instead.
- Remove and re-energise once a year; replace a cracked stone.

## 6. Remedies around health

Give the planetary remedy (mantra, daan, worship, gemstone if the planet is a functional benefic)
as support. Remedies go alongside the treatment the person is receiving - never ask anyone to stop
or delay medicine, surgery or hospital care for a remedy.

## 7. Prescription format

> **Shukra (yogakaraka, kamzor - asta 6°, Vimshopaka 8.2):** Heera 0.5-0.75 ct ya safed pukhraj
> 4-5 ct, chandi/platinum mein, madhyama ungli, Shukravar subah Shukra hora mein, "Om Draam Dreem
> Draum Sah Shukraya Namah" 108 baar jap kar dharan karein. Saath mein Shukravar ko chawal/ghee ka
> daan, Shri Suktam path.
>
> **Shani (8th lord, Sade Sati chal rahi):** ratna nahi. Shanivar ko Hanuman Chalisa, til-tel ka
> diya, 23,000 Shani beej mantra ka anushthan, shramikon ko kambal daan.
