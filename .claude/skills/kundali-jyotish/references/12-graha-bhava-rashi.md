# Planets in houses and in signs

Base meanings for combining with dignity, lordship, aspects and dasha. Natural benefics generally
improve the house they sit in; natural malefics do well in 3, 6, 10, 11 and harm the significations
of other houses (while often giving drive or worldly success).

## Planets in signs (by dignity and element)

- **Exalted** (Sun Aries, Moon Taurus, Mars Capricorn, Mercury Virgo, Jupiter Cancer, Venus Pisces,
  Saturn Libra): the planet's best qualities; its houses flourish.
- **Moolatrikona / own sign**: confident, stable delivery of the planet's significations.
- **Friend's sign**: comfortable, cooperative results; **enemy's sign**: friction, effort needed.
- **Debilitated** (opposite of exaltation): weakness in its significations unless neecha bhanga.

Sign flavour by element (applies to any planet placed there):
- Fire (Aries, Leo, Sagittarius): action, leadership, dharma, ego.
- Earth (Taurus, Virgo, Capricorn): practical, material, steady, service.
- Air (Gemini, Libra, Aquarius): intellect, relationships, ideas, networks.
- Water (Cancer, Scorpio, Pisces): emotions, intuition, depth, spirituality.

Specific combinations worth remembering:
- Sun: Aries brilliant leader; Leo royal; Libra (debilitated) ego and authority clashes; Capricorn/
  Aquarius (Saturn's signs) duty, delayed recognition.
- Moon: Taurus/Cancer calm, nurturing; Scorpio (debilitated) emotional intensity; Gemini/Virgo
  analytical mind; Sagittarius/Pisces devotional mind.
- Mars: Aries/Scorpio/Capricorn strong, effective; Cancer (debilitated) suppressed anger,
  property issues; Gemini/Virgo argumentative.
- Mercury: Gemini/Virgo sharp intellect; Pisces (debilitated) intuitive but scattered; Sagittarius
  philosophical.
- Jupiter: Cancer/Sagittarius/Pisces wise and fortunate; Capricorn (debilitated) practical faith,
  wisdom tested; Gemini/Virgo (Mercury's signs) knowledge but doubts.
- Venus: Taurus/Libra/Pisces artistic, happy relationships; Virgo (debilitated) critical in love;
  Leo/Cancer emotional, dramatic.
- Saturn: Libra/Capricorn/Aquarius disciplined success; Aries (debilitated) frustration, impulsive
  hard work; Leo/Cancer conflict with authority/home.
- Rahu: Taurus/Gemini/Virgo/Aquarius gives worldly success (commonly cited); Scorpio/Sagittarius
  troubled. Ketu: Scorpio/Sagittarius/Pisces spiritual strength.
