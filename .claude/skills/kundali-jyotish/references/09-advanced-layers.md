## 1. Friendship (Panchadha maitri) - §12

- **Naisargika (natural)** is permanent. **Tatkalika (temporary)**: planets in 2, 3, 4, 10, 11, 12
  from a planet are its temporary friends; 1, 5, 6, 7, 8, 9 are temporary enemies.
- **Panchadha (compound)**: natural friend + temporary friend = Adhimitra (great friend);
  friend + enemy = Sama; neutral + friend = Mitra; neutral + enemy = Shatru; enemy + friend = Sama;
  enemy + enemy = Adhishatru (great enemy).
- Use it for:
  - **Dignity**: a planet in a great friend's sign gives ~75% of its good results, in a great
    enemy's sign ~25% and with friction.
  - **Conjunctions**: compound friends cooperate (their combined significations flourish);
    compound enemies in one sign fight (the weaker one's significations suffer).
  - **Dasha**: MD lord and AD lord as compound friends give smooth periods; enemies give friction,
    even if both are benefic. Read the row of the MD lord, column of the AD lord.
  - **Dispositor**: a planet whose dispositor is its great enemy delivers late or with struggle.
- The table is asymmetric (Sun may treat Saturn as enemy while Saturn's view of Sun differs);
  always read from the acting planet's row.

## 2. Aspect strength (sphuta drishti) - §13

Whole-sign aspects (§3) say *whether* a planet aspects; virupas say *how much*.
| Virupas | Strength | Meaning |
|---|---|---|
| 45-60 | full / three-quarter | decisive influence |
| 30-45 | half to three-quarter | clearly felt |
| 15-30 | quarter to half | noticeable |
| < 15 | negligible | ignore |

- Benefic aspect (Jupiter, Venus, Mercury, waxing Moon) on a planet/house protects and expands it;
  malefic aspect (Saturn, Mars, Sun, Rahu/Ketu by sign) restricts or damages - judged also by the
  aspecting planet's functional role (a yogakaraka Saturn's aspect builds).
- **Aspect on bhava madhya** (house centre) tells which houses are most influenced. Jupiter 45+ on
  the 7th madhya strongly supports marriage; Saturn 45+ on the 10th madhya = career through hard,
  slow work.
- **Drik bala** (§15): net benefic minus malefic aspect received / 4. Positive = protected planet;
  strongly negative = afflicted planet (its significations suffer in its dasha).
- **Conjunction orbs**: < 1° exact (planets fuse; also graha yuddha for Mars-Saturn group), < 5°
  close, beyond that a looser blend. Moon/Sun with Rahu/Ketu within 5° = strong grahan effect.

## 3. Avasthas - §11

| System | States | Use |
|---|---|---|
| Baladi (by degree; reversed in even signs) | Bala 25%, Kumara 50%, Yuva 100%, Vriddha ~10%, Mrita ~0% | how much of its result the planet can deliver |
| Jagradadi (by dignity) | Jagrat (awake: exalted/own/MT) full; Swapna (dreaming: friend/neutral) medium; Sushupti (asleep: enemy/debilitated) little | whether the planet "acts" |
| Deeptadi (by dignity/state) | Deepta (exalted) brilliant success; Swastha (own/MT) comfort and fame; Mudita (great friend) joy; Shanta (friend) peace; Dina (neutral) dependence; Duhkhita (enemy) sorrow; Khala (great enemy) wickedness/loss; Vikala (debilitated) weakness; Kopa (combust) anger/loss | the *quality* of results in its dasha |

A planet that is Yuva + Jagrat + Deepta/Swastha gives its full promise; Mrita + Sushupti + Khala
gives little even if it forms a yoga.

## 4. Nakshatra deity, nature and tara - §11

- **Deity** colours the planet's expression (Jupiter in Bharani - Yama: discipline, judgement;
  Venus in Purva Phalguni - Bhaga: pleasure, romance, luxury).
- **Nature** (for muhurta and temperament): Dhruva fixed (stable starts, property, marriage),
  Chara movable (travel, vehicles), Ugra fierce (conflict, surgery), Mishra mixed, Kshipra swift
  (trade, medicine, learning), Mridu soft (arts, friendship, romance), Tikshna sharp (separation,
  occult, decisive action).
- **Tara from janma nakshatra (Navatara)**: planets in Vipat (3), Pratyari (5) or Naidhana (7)
  tara from the Moon's nakshatra create obstacles in their dashas; Sampat (2), Kshema (4), Sadhaka
  (6), Mitra (8), Parama Mitra (9) help. Janma (1) = intense, personal.

## 5. Flags - §11

Sandhi (first/last degree of a sign: weak, unsettled results), Gandanta (water-fire junction:
crisis-then-transformation), Vargottama (stable, reliable), War with X (the loser - usually the
one further south / smaller - loses strength), Retrograde (vakri: strong, results come with
repetition, review or reversal), Combust (asta: significations hidden or burnt; Mercury mildly,
Venus/Jupiter/Saturn more).

## 6. Bhava Chalit - §14

Rashi (whole-sign) houses give the sign/dignity context; Sripati chalit shows which house a planet
actually *delivers* in. When a planet shifts (e.g. rashi 10th -> chalit 9th), its house results
come from the chalit house while its sign dignity stays. Planets near a bhava sandhi give mixed
results of both houses. KP bhava (§5) is the third view for event questions.

## 7. Strength - §15

| Component | Max | Reading |
|---|---|---|
| Uchcha bala | 60 | 60 at deep exaltation, 0 at deep debilitation |
| Dig bala | 60 | directional: Sun/Mars 10th, Jupiter/Mercury 1st, Moon/Venus 4th, Saturn 7th |
| Naisargika | 60 | fixed natural strength (Sun 60 ... Saturn 8.6) |
| Paksha | 60 | benefics strong in bright fortnight, malefics in dark |
| Saptavargaja | 315 | dignity across D1, D2, D3, D7, D9, D12, D30 (>110 good, <80 weak) |
| Chesta | 60 | motion: retrograde 60, fast 45, mean 30, slow/stationary 15 (approximate) |
| Drik | +/- | net aspects received |
| Vimshopaka | 20 | dignity across 16 vargas: 15+ excellent, 10-15 good, 5-10 average, <5 weak |

The printed order ranks planets by these components. Use it to decide which planet "wins" when
indications conflict, and which weak benefic needs a remedy. If the report has full Shadbala in
rupas with the required minimum (Sun 6.5, Moon 6, Mars 5, Mercury 7, Jupiter 6.5, Venus 5.5,
Saturn 5), use it as the final word.

## 8. Special lagnas - §16

- **Hora Lagna (HL)**: wealth and status. Planets in/aspecting HL and its lord's strength show
  money flow. HL lord in a kendra/trikona from Lagna = earning power.
- **Ghati Lagna (GL)**: power, authority, position. Lagna lord, HL lord and GL lord connected =
  Raj yoga of status.
- **Bhava Lagna (BL)**: the self's expression; secondary ascendant.
- **Indu Lagna**: wealth sign. Benefics in it or in kendras from it give steady wealth; the dasha
  of a planet in the Indu Lagna brings money. Malefics there give wealth by hard means.
- **Sree Lagna**: Lakshmi's point - prosperity and grace; benefic connection = fortune.

## 9. Upagrahas - §16

- **Gulika / Mandi** (Saturn's son): the most malefic sensitive point. Its house suffers (in 2nd:
  family/speech troubles; in 7th: marital friction; in 10th: career setbacks; in 3/6/11: good, it
  destroys enemies). Planets conjunct Gulika are afflicted. The dasha of Gulika's sign lord and
  Saturn/Rahu transits over Gulika bring difficulties. Some schools use the start (Gulika), some
  the middle (Mandi) of Saturn's portion; both are printed.
- **Dhuma, Vyatipata, Parivesha, Indrachapa, Upaketu** (Sun-based): malefic sub-points; when in
  Lagna or with the Moon or Sun they add affliction (Dhuma: heat/fire; Vyatipata: accidents;
  Parivesha: obstacles; Indrachapa: deception; Upaketu: sudden loss). Mention only when they sit
  on Lagna, Moon, Sun or a key house lord.

## 10. Yogini dasha - §17

Mangala (Moon, 1y) success and comfort · Pingala (Sun, 2y) mixed, health/ego issues · Dhanya
(Jupiter, 3y) wealth and growth · Bhramari (Mars, 4y) travel, restlessness, disputes · Bhadrika
(Mercury, 5y) good fortune, learning, friends · Ulka (Saturn, 6y) hardship, labour, losses ·
Siddha (Venus, 7y) success, comforts, marriage · Sankata (Rahu, 8y) crises and upheaval. Judge
each also by its lord's natal strength. When Yogini and Vimshottari point the same way, promote the
prediction one strength level.

## 11. Prastarashtakavarga and kakshya - §18

For Jupiter and Saturn the table shows which of the 8 givers put a bindu in each sign. When a
transiting Jupiter/Saturn passes through the 3°45' kakshya of a giver marked 1, those weeks give
good results; through a 0 kakshya, weak or bad ones. Combine with the planet's BAV total in that
sign (§8): 4+ bindus = good transit; 0-2 = difficult.

## 12. Exact transit hits - files 02, 03, 06

Lists the exact dates when Jupiter, Saturn, Rahu and Ketu (and Mars in the 7-day file) conjoin or
aspect (by their special aspects) natal planets, Lagna and MC. Effects build 1-2° before the exact
date. A retrograde planet can hit the same degree 3 times - the first hit starts the matter, the
second tests it, the third settles it. Use these dates to narrow a dasha-level prediction to weeks:
e.g. "Shani 7th-aspect on natal Venus 18 Apr 2027 -> relationship test around that month".

## 13. Putting it together

For each prediction, list the factors that agree: natal promise (dignity, house, yoga, bala,
varga), dasha (Vimshottari + Yogini, friendship of MD-AD lords), transit (house from Lagna/Moon,
BAV/SAV, kakshya, exact hits). The more independent layers agree, the higher the strength label.
